🎣 Phishing Awareness

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals pretend to be trusted organizations such as banks, shopping websites, or government agencies to steal personal information.

They often send fake emails, SMS messages, or create fake websites that look genuine.

Warning Signs
  • Unknown sender email address
  • Urgent requests asking you to act immediately
  • Requests for passwords or OTPs
  • Spelling and grammar mistakes
  • Unexpected attachments
  • Suspicious links
  • Offers that seem too good to be true
Stay Safe
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Verify website URLs carefully.
  • Report suspicious emails.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication.

🌐 Safe Browsing Tips

Browse Smart. Stay Protected.

Safe browsing habits protect your personal information from hackers, malware, and fake websites.
  • Always use HTTPS websites.
  • Keep browsers updated.
  • Avoid clicking pop-up advertisements.
  • Download software only from official websites.
  • Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking.
  • Use trusted antivirus software.
  • Clear browsing data regularly.
  • Verify websites before entering personal information.

🔒 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Add an Extra Layer of Protection

Two-Factor Authentication requires two methods of verification before allowing access to your account.

Even if someone steals your password, they still cannot log in without the second verification.

Examples
  • Password + OTP
  • Password + Authenticator App
  • Password + Fingerprint
  • Password + Face Recognition
Benefits
  • Better account security
  • Protection from stolen passwords
  • Safer online banking
  • Reduces hacking attempts

📧 Email Security Tips

Keep Your Inbox Secure

Email is one of the most common methods used by attackers to spread malware and steal information.
  • Verify the sender before opening emails.
  • Do not click unknown links.
  • Do not download suspicious attachments.
  • Enable spam filters.
  • Never share OTPs through email.
  • Use strong passwords for email accounts.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
  • Delete suspicious emails immediately.

🔐 Password Security

🛡️ Secure Your Digital Identity

Strong passwords are your first line of defense against cyber attacks. Weak passwords can expose your email accounts, banking details, social media profiles, and other sensitive information.

Always create unique passwords for different accounts and avoid personal information such as your name, birth date, or mobile number.

Regularly update passwords and enable Two-Factor Authentication whenever available.

Learn How to Create a Strong Password

  • ✅ Minimum 8 characters (12+ recommended)
  • ✅ Include uppercase letters (A-Z)
  • ✅ Include lowercase letters (a-z)
  • ✅ Include numbers (0-9)
  • ✅ Include special characters (! @ # $ % ^ & *)
  • ✅ Avoid dictionary words
  • ✅ Avoid names and birthdays
  • ✅ Use different passwords for different accounts
  • ✅ Enable Two-Factor Authentication
  • ✅ Change passwords regularly
