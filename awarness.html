🛡️ Why Cyber Awareness Matters

Why Cyber Awareness Matters

Every day millions of people browse the internet, shop online, use banking apps, and communicate through social media.

Cyber criminals look for small mistakes rather than advanced computers. A single careless click can expose personal information, passwords, and financial data.

Learning basic cyber safety habits helps protect your identity, your money, and your digital future.

Benefits of Cyber Awareness
  • Protects your personal information
  • Prevents online fraud and scams
  • Keeps your devices secure
  • Reduces the risk of identity theft
  • Promotes safe online behavior
  • Builds confidence while using the internet

⚠️ Common Online Threats

Common Online Threats

Cyber criminals use different types of attacks to steal information, damage devices, or trick users into giving away personal details.

Types of Online Threats
  • 🦠 Malware: Harmful software that steals information, damages files, or slows your computer.
  • 💻 Ransomware: Encrypts your files and demands payment to restore access.
  • 👤 Identity Theft: Criminals misuse your personal information for fraud.
  • 🎭 Social Engineering: Attackers manipulate people into revealing confidential information.
  • 💳 Online Fraud: Fake shopping websites and payment scams designed to steal money.
  • 📱 Mobile Scams: Fake apps and messages that target smartphone users.
How to Stay Safe
  • Install trusted security software.
  • Never download files from unknown sources.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links.
  • Verify websites before making payments.

🛡️ Daily Cyber Safety Habits

Daily Cyber Safety Habits

Practicing good cyber hygiene every day helps protect your devices, personal information, and online accounts from hackers and cyber threats.

Essential Safety Habits
  • 🔄 Keep your operating system and software updated.
  • 💾 Backup important files regularly.
  • 🔒 Review and manage your privacy settings.
  • 📱 Lock your devices with a strong password or biometric authentication.
  • 🛡️ Install and regularly update antivirus software.
  • ☁️ Use secure cloud storage to back up important data.
Additional Tips
  • Use strong and unique passwords for every account.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • Avoid downloading files from unknown sources.
  • Log out of accounts when using public computers.

❌ Myths vs ✅ Facts

Common Cyber Security Myths

Many people believe false information about cyber security, making them more vulnerable to online attacks. Knowing the facts helps you stay protected.

Myths vs Facts
  • ❌ Myth: Only big companies are attacked.
    ✅ Fact: Anyone connected to the internet can become a victim.
  • ❌ Myth: Antivirus alone keeps you completely safe.
    ✅ Fact: Safe browsing habits are equally important.
  • ❌ Myth: Hackers only steal money.
    ✅ Fact: They may steal personal data, photos, and online accounts too.

🚨 If You Think Your Account Has Been Hacked

What You Should Do

If you believe your account or device has been compromised, act immediately to reduce damage and protect your information.

Emergency Steps
  • 1️⃣ Disconnect your device from the internet if malware is suspected.
  • 2️⃣ Change all important passwords using a trusted device.
  • 3️⃣ Run a complete antivirus or malware scan.
  • 4️⃣ Report the incident to your bank or affected online service.
