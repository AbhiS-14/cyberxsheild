Welcome to Cyber Sheild

Cyber Sheild is a Cyber Security Awareness Portal developed to educate users about online safety and digital security. As cyber attacks continue to increase around the world, understanding cyber security has become an essential skill for everyone.

Our portal provides educational content together with interactive tools that help users create strong passwords, understand phishing attacks, browse safely, and improve their overall cyber security knowledge.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to spread cyber security awareness and encourage responsible internet usage. We believe every internet user should understand how to protect personal information and stay safe from cyber threats.

🌍 Why Cyber Security Matters

Every day millions of cyber attacks target individuals, businesses and organizations. Weak passwords, fake websites, malware, ransomware and phishing attacks can lead to financial loss and identity theft.

By learning simple cyber security practices such as creating strong passwords, enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), keeping software updated and avoiding suspicious links, everyone can reduce their chances of becoming a victim.

🚀 Features of Cyber Sheild

🔐 Password Checker

Checks password strength based on length, uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters. It also gives a strength score.

🔑 Password Generator

Generates random secure passwords using customizable options like uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols.

🧠 Cyber Security Quiz

Interactive quiz that helps users test their cyber security knowledge through multiple-choice questions.

🎣 Phishing Awareness

Learn how to identify phishing emails, fake websites, and fraudulent messages. Understand common warning signs and protect yourself from online scams.

🌐 Safe Browsing Tips

Discover best practices for safe internet browsing, including verifying websites, avoiding malicious downloads, and browsing securely over HTTPS.

🔒 Two-Factor Authentication

Understand how Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of protection to your online accounts using OTPs, authenticator apps, or biometrics.

📧 Email Security Tips

Learn how to recognize suspicious emails, avoid malicious attachments, identify fake senders, and keep your email account secure.
